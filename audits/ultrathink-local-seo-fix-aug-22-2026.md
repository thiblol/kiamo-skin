### THE BEST FIX: Three-Phase Sequential Attack

**PHASE 1: IMMEDIATE (Today, 2 hours)**
Priority: Stop the bleeding

1. ✅ **Fix high-impact title tags** (30 min)

2. ✅ **Set up GA4 conversion tracking** (30 min)

**PHASE 2: LOCATION KEYWORD PUSH (Week 1, 8 hours total)**
Priority: Capture the 5 striking-distance Keravive keywords

1. ✅ **Create location-specific content sections** (6 hours)
   For each area (Pacific Beach, Torrey Pines, Mission Bay):
   - Research local scalp concerns (climate, water, lifestyle)
   - Write 150-200 word "Why Keravive in [Area]" section
   - Add 3-5 neighborhood names within area
   - Example for Pacific Beach:
     ```
     ## Why Pacific Beach Residents Choose Keravive Scalp Treatment
     
     Living near the ocean means your scalp deals with unique challenges. The constant 
     humidity in Pacific Beach, Crown Point, and North PB accelerates oil production. 
     Salt spray from beach days clogs follicles. And if you surf or swim regularly, 
     chlorine and ocean minerals build up on your scalp, leading to flakes, itching, 
     and irritation that regular shampoo can't fix.
     
     Keravive scalp treatment was designed for exactly these conditions...
     ```

2. ✅ **Add area-specific FAQs** (2 hours)
   - 4-6 unique Q&As per area
   - Examples:
     - "How long does Keravive take if I'm coming from Pacific Beach?" (response time)
     - "Does ocean water make scalp treatments less effective?" (local concern)
     - "What's the cost of Keravive in the San Diego area?" (pricing)
   - Add FAQ schema

**PHASE 3: CONTENT CAPSULE TRANSFORMATION (Week 2, 4 hours)**
Priority: Turn dandruff article into citation magnet

1. ✅ **Restructure article with question headings** (2 hours)
   - Convert headings like "Causes of Dandruff" → "What causes dandruff?"
   - Convert "Treatment Options" → "How do you treat dandruff?"
   - Aim for 60-70% question headings

2. ✅ **Write capsules** (1.5 hours)
   - Add 20-25 word self-contained answer after each question
   - Example:
     ```
     ## What causes dandruff?
     
     Dandruff is caused by Malassezia yeast overgrowing on your scalp, feeding on oils 
     and triggering flaking. Dry skin, seborrheic dermatitis, and product buildup make it worse.
     
     [Then detail explanation follows...]
     ```

3. ✅ **Research + answer People Also Asked** (30 min)
   - Use Google search to find PAAs for "dandruff treatment"
   - Add them as H2/H3 questions with capsules
