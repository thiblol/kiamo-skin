## 3. Target Reader

Someone with dark marks, melasma, post-acne scarring or barrier damage who has already tried something that didn't work — usually a drugstore product or a peel elsewhere that made things worse.

**Three fears to address directly in any content:**
1. Treatment will darken the pigmentation, not lighten it — this fear is well-founded, meet it head-on
2. The practitioner has not treated skin like theirs before
3. Spending hundreds of dollars and seeing nothing

Melanin-rich skin is KIAMO's verified, uncopyable differentiator. Address it in the first 100 words of every page.

1. **No pronouns.** She/her/hers/he/him/his — never. Write: "Kiki brings…" / "The studio…"

3. **No Del Mar address.** The Del Mar studio is closed. Must not appear anywhere, including schema.
4. **No surname.** Kiki only — never Michaux, never Brown.
5. **No invented prices.** Only the 9 verified prices above, or "Pricing at consultation."
6. **No impossible service claims.** No lasers, IPL, injectables, prescriptions, microneedling by pen.
7. **Title tag ≤ 60 characters. Meta description ≤ 155.**
8. **Exactly one H1 per page.**
9. **CTA says "Request Appointment"** — never "Book Instantly" or "Book Now."
10. **No keyword lists in visible copy.** Keywords go in real sentences or `<title>` only.

## 7. On-Page SEO Rules (Geo-Modifier System)

Reverse-engineered from the #1 local pack holder in a comparable market. Apply to every page.

### The 6-slot rule — where geography goes, then stops

| Slot | Geo | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `<title>` | YES | `HydraFacial in San Diego \| KIAMO Skin` |
| `<meta description>` | YES | `HydraFacial in Mission Hills, San Diego. From $259.` |
| `<h1>` | **BARE — no geo** | `The foundation of healthy skin.` |
| Deck line under H1 | YES | `HydraFacial in San Diego — cleanse, extract, infuse.` |
| Opening paragraph | YES | `Looking for a HydraFacial in San Diego?…` |
| One H2 — the pricing one | YES | `Pricing in San Diego` |
| Body copy, FAQs, alt text | **NO GEO** | Plain English |

H1 exception: area pages, GBP category pages, and homepage get a geo H1. Everywhere else: bare.

### Opening paragraph pattern (every treatment and area page)

Restate the query as a normal sentence, then carry a fact only that page can state:

- HydraFacial: `Looking for a HydraFacial in San Diego? KIAMO SKIN is a licensed corrective skin studio in Mission Hills, five minutes from Hillcrest and Little Italy.`
- Chemical peel: `Looking for a chemical peel in San Diego that is safe on melanin-rich skin? That is the work this studio was built around.`
- Dermaplaning: `Looking for dermaplaning in San Diego? The dermaplane facial at KIAMO SKIN is $199, performed by a licensed esthetician in Mission Hills.`

### Geo anchor text on hub pages (highest-value move)

Hub pages use geo-modified link labels. Footers and sibling blocks do not.

- `/treatments` hub: `HydraFacial in San Diego` · `chemical peel in San Diego` · `microneedling in San Diego`
- `/service-areas` hub: `Facials in La Jolla` · `Facials in Chula Vista` etc.
- Footer links: **bare** — `HydraFacial`, never `HydraFacial in San Diego`

### Targets

- Geo-sentence density in main content: **10–12%** (currently 6%)
- Geo anchor text on hub pages: **~40%** of internal links (currently 0%)
- Titles carrying a geo term: **100%** of pages except `/404`
- Alt text geo: **0%** — accessibility attribute, never stuffed

## 9. Journal / Blog Strategy

Journal is the only place for informational content. Every article must:
- Answer one specific real question (from PAA data below)
- Be 100% unique — no sentence survives a city/topic find-and-replace
- Include one soft CTA at the **end only**, after value is delivered
- Link internally to 2–3 treatment pages with geo-modified anchor text
- Hit 10–12% geo-sentence density in body (0% in FAQ answers)
- Carry `BlogPosting` + `BreadcrumbList` schema

### Three San Diego-specific angles no competitor covers

1. **June Gloom** — marine layer still passes UV 8–10. Links to pigmentation, barrier, SPF habits.
2. **Hard water (16 grains/gallon)** — mineral film, scalp flaking, buildup. Links to keravive, DMK.
3. **Santa Ana season** — sudden dry wind causes barrier collapse. Links to peel timing, DMK.

### PAA-sourced article candidates

- Why June Gloom still damages your skin in San Diego → `lira-peels`, `hydrafacial`
- What San Diego's hard water does to your scalp → `keravive-scalp`
- How to choose a chemical peel for melanin-rich skin → `lira-peels`, `dmk-enzyme-therapy`
- Is dermaplaning safe for darker skin tones? → `dermaplaning`, `lira-peels`
- How many Procell microchanneling sessions does acne scarring take? → `procell-microchanneling`
- What is the difference between a head spa and scalp therapy? → `keravive-scalp`
- How much does a facial cost in San Diego? → `hydrafacial`, `treatments`
